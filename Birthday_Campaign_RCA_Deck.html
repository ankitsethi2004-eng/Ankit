Why did conversion improve? Hypothesis · evidence-backed

  • Free “birthday gift with every order” offer & messaging — the single biggest changed variable. Gift attach went 19% → 99.9% of orders. 40–55%
  • CRM blitz (WhatsApp O3+/O1, Push, Email) drove ₹1.16M click-through / 353 orders of warm, ready-to-buy intent. 20–30%
  • Paid-media efficiency — Meta ROAS +24%, Google +33% at only +21% spend. Better offer converted the same clicks. 15–25%
  • New premium SKU launches (Qi2 power-banks, 0 → ₹630k) gave high-AOV reasons to buy & qualify. 5–10%
Sub-drivers overlap — offer, CRM & paid jointly produced the conversion lift and can’t be cleanly split without a holdout test.

What Worked · Playbook · Next Tests

Repeat the demand engine — fix the margin leak

✅ What worked — ranked

  • Universal “free birthday gift” offer + messaging — biggest conversion lever.
  • CRM blitz — WhatsApp O3+/O1 the workhorse (CTR 8–10%, ROMS 3.2); warm-intent fuel + paid amplifier.
  • More efficient paid media — ROAS ↑ at scaled spend, not just bigger budgets.
  • ₹4,499 threshold + new premium power-banks — basket trade-up engine (~10–16% of growth).
  • Did NOT work as levers: basket size, AOV, discounting — by design there was no sitewide discount.
Margin flag: a gift on 99.9% of orders is unbudgeted COGS and kills the trade-up incentive. Re-gate it to a threshold.

🎂 Birthday Playbook + experiments

  • Keep the gift as a CVR device — but re-anchor to a threshold (test ₹2,999 vs ₹4,499) so it lifts conversion and AOV.
  • Lead with WhatsApp O3+/O1 to warm audiences, then scale paid into proven demand. Cut Email-to-O1/RNB (11% open) & Push Mystery-Gift-2.
  • Time premium SKU launches just above the threshold; add a “₹X more unlocks your gift” cart nudge.
  • Always report paid units/order & same-weekday — never let ₹0 gift lines fake basket growth.
Next-sale tests: (1) geo/audience holdout to measure gift incrementality · (2) threshold A/B for the CVR-vs-AOV-vs-margin optimum · (3) CRM suppression holdout · (4) connect GA4 channel + funnel + user-type to close the New-vs-Returning / ATC-vs-checkout gaps.
